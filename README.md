Key Insights:
Peak sales occur during Friday evenings and weekends.

Latte is the most popular product, while Espresso is the least.

Majority of customers prefer card payments (~70%).

Afternoon and evening are the most profitable time periods.

Business Recommendations:
Hire additional staff during peak hours to meet customer demand.

Offer bundle promotions pairing low-selling products with top sellers.

Launch time-limited offers for low-demand drinks.

Optimize marketing around peak times to maximize profits.
